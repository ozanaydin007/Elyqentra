How the tracker works

Create an account and answer one question: what is your goal? The tracker recommends a workout split and a diet that fit it, then calculates your daily calories and macros from your height, weight, age and activity level.

Your split starts on the day you choose it. Each day you see the exact exercises, sets and reps. Mark the workout done and the plan moves on. If you skip it, or simply do not log it, the session moves to the next day and the rest of the cycle follows. Rest days stay where they belong in the cycle, so your recovery is never squeezed out.

You can switch to any of the 10 splits and 12 diets at any time, and log your meals from a database of 116 common foods.

Goals you can choose

  • Maximum muscle gain: Build as much lean muscle as possible.
  • Maximum mass gain: Get bigger and heavier fast, accepting some fat gain.
  • Gain weight: Steadily put on healthy weight.
  • Maximum fat loss: Lose fat as fast as is sustainable while keeping muscle.
  • Get lean and defined: Lose fat and build muscle at the same time.
  • Build strength: Lift heavier on the big barbell lifts.
  • Strength and endurance: Be strong and fit for running or HYROX.
  • Maintain and stay healthy: Keep your current weight and feel good.

Diet plans

Each plan includes calorie and macro guidance, what to eat and a sample day.

Clean Bulk

A modest calorie surplus from mostly whole foods to build muscle while limiting fat gain.

Dirty Bulk

A large calorie surplus with flexible food choices to gain size and weight as fast as possible.

Cutting (Definition)

A moderate calorie deficit with high protein to lose fat and keep muscle.

Weight Maintenance

Eat at maintenance calories to hold your weight steady while training.

Body Recomposition

A small deficit with very high protein to lose fat and gain muscle at the same time.

Healthy Balanced Diet

A flexible, whole-food diet that supports training, health and long-term consistency.

Keto Diet

Very low carbohydrate, high fat eating that shifts the body into ketosis.

Vegetarian Diet

Meat-free eating with dairy and eggs, built to hit protein targets for training.

Vegan Diet

Fully plant-based eating planned for complete protein and key nutrients.

Mediterranean Diet

Olive oil, fish, vegetables and legumes: one of the most studied healthy diets.

High-Protein Diet

Protein-first eating for strength athletes and anyone who wants to stay full.

Intermittent Fasting 16:8

Eat all daily food within an 8-hour window, then fast for 16 hours.

The content on Elyqentra, including workout programs, diet plans, calorie and macro calculations, nutrition data and articles, is for general information and education only. It is not medical advice and does not replace diagnosis or treatment by a qualified health professional.

Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise or nutrition program, especially if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have a heart, kidney, metabolic or other medical condition, take medication, have a history of injury, or have ever struggled with disordered eating.

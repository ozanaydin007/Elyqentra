Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-26

Account and tracker data

When you create an account, your username, a salted hash of your password, your profile (goal, body measurements, chosen split and diet) and your logs are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. This data is not sent to our servers. Clearing your browser data deletes it, and you can delete your account at any time from the tracker settings.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message before personalized ads are used.

Analytics and logs

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and pages requested for security and performance.

Children

Elyqentra is not directed at children under 16.

Your rights and contact

For any privacy question or request, contact info@elyqentra.com.

Keto Diet

Very low carbohydrate, high fat eating that shifts the body into ketosis.

The ketogenic diet keeps net carbohydrates around 20-30 g per day, which pushes the body to produce ketones from fat. Many people find it reduces appetite.

Fat loss on keto still comes from a calorie deficit. High-intensity training performance may drop, especially in the first weeks. People with diabetes, kidney issues or who take medication should talk to a doctor before starting.

Calories and macros

CaloriesUses the calorie target from your goal: surplus to gain, deficit to lose, maintenance to hold.
Protein1.8 g per kg of bodyweight (0.82 g per lb)
FatThe remaining calories after protein and carbs
CarbohydratesUnder about 25 g net carbs per day

What to eat

  • Meat, fish and eggs
  • Low-carb vegetables like spinach, broccoli and zucchini
  • Avocado, olive oil, butter and nuts
  • Cheese and full-fat yogurt in moderation

What to limit

  • Bread, rice, pasta and potatoes
  • Sugar and most fruit
  • Beer and sweet drinks

Sample day

BreakfastEggs scrambled in butter with spinach and cheese
LunchChicken thigh salad with avocado and olive oil
SnackAlmonds or a cheese stick
DinnerSalmon with broccoli and garlic butter

Cutting (Definition)

A moderate calorie deficit with high protein to lose fat and keep muscle.

Cutting, often called getting defined, means eating below maintenance so the body uses stored fat. A deficit of about 500 calories a day typically leads to around 0.5 kg (1 lb) of loss per week.

Protein is set high because it protects muscle in a deficit and keeps you full. Keep lifting heavy: the training signal is what tells your body to hold on to muscle. Avoid extreme deficits; they cost muscle, energy and mood.

Calories and macros

CaloriesAbout 500 calories below maintenance per day.
Protein2.2 g per kg of bodyweight (1.0 g per lb)
Fat25% of calories
CarbohydratesThe remaining calories after protein and fat

What to eat

  • Lean protein at every meal
  • High-volume vegetables
  • Potatoes, oats and fruit for carbs
  • Measured portions of fats

What to limit

  • Liquid calories
  • Mindless snacking
  • Calorie-dense sauces

Sample day

BreakfastEgg-white omelet with spinach, 1 whole egg, toast
LunchTurkey breast salad with chickpeas and light dressing
SnackSkyr or Greek yogurt with berries
DinnerWhite fish, potatoes and roasted vegetables
EveningProtein shake or cottage cheese

Clean Bulk

A modest calorie surplus from mostly whole foods to build muscle while limiting fat gain.

A clean bulk, also called a lean bulk, puts you about 250-350 calories above maintenance. That is enough to support muscle growth without the rapid fat gain of a large surplus.

Aim to gain roughly 0.25-0.5% of your bodyweight per week. If the scale climbs faster than that for two weeks in a row, take 100-150 calories off.

Calories and macros

CaloriesAbout 300 calories above maintenance per day.
Protein1.8 g per kg of bodyweight (0.82 g per lb)
Fat25% of calories
CarbohydratesThe remaining calories after protein and fat

What to eat

  • Lean meats, fish and eggs
  • Rice, oats, potatoes and whole-grain bread
  • Fruit and vegetables at most meals
  • Olive oil, nuts and nut butters

What to limit

  • Fried food and pastries
  • Sugary drinks
  • Alcohol

Sample day

BreakfastOats with milk, banana and peanut butter; 3 eggs
LunchChicken breast, rice, mixed vegetables and olive oil
SnackGreek yogurt with berries and honey
DinnerSalmon, potatoes and a large salad
Before bedCottage cheese or a casein shake

Portions depend on your calorie target. Use the tracker to see your exact numbers, and check nutrition values in the food database.

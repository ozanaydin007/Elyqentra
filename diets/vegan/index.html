Vegan Diet

Fully plant-based eating planned for complete protein and key nutrients.

A vegan diet excludes all animal products. Muscle can be built on it with good planning: combine several protein sources through the day and aim slightly higher on total protein.

Vitamin B12 must come from fortified foods or a supplement. Vitamin D, iodine, omega-3, iron and zinc are also worth checking with a professional.

Calories and macros

CaloriesUses the calorie target from your goal: surplus to gain, deficit to lose, maintenance to hold.
Protein1.8 g per kg of bodyweight (0.82 g per lb)
Fat27% of calories
CarbohydratesThe remaining calories after protein and fat

What to eat

  • Tofu, tempeh, seitan and edamame
  • Lentils, beans and chickpeas
  • Pea or soy protein powder
  • Whole grains, nuts and seeds

What to limit

  • Relying on processed vegan junk food
  • Single-source protein days

Sample day

BreakfastTofu scramble with whole-grain toast
LunchChickpea and quinoa bowl with tahini
SnackSoy yogurt with granola, or a pea protein shake
DinnerTempeh with brown rice and broccoli

Portions depend on your calorie target. Use the tracker to see your exact numbers, and check nutrition values in the food database.

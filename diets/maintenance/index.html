Weight Maintenance

Eat at maintenance calories to hold your weight steady while training.

Maintenance means eating about as many calories as you burn. It is the right phase after a long diet, during a busy period, or when you are simply happy with your current body.

Muscle can still be built slowly at maintenance, especially for beginners, as long as protein is adequate and training is progressive.

Who this diet is for

Maintenance is for people happy with their current weight, anyone coming off a diet who needs a break, and people going through stressful or busy periods. It is also a sensible phase between a bulk and a cut.

Why it works

Eating at maintenance removes the stress of a deficit and limits the fat gain of a surplus. Training quality, sleep and mood often improve compared with dieting.

Beginners and people returning after a break can still build noticeable muscle at maintenance as long as protein is adequate and training is progressive.

Calories and macros

CaloriesMaintenance calories.
Protein1.6 g per kg of bodyweight (0.73 g per lb)
Fat30% of calories
CarbohydratesThe remaining calories after protein and fat

What to eat

  • A balanced mix of protein, carbs and fats
  • Plenty of fruit and vegetables
  • Foods you enjoy, in sensible portions

What to limit

  • Large swings between strict days and binge days

How to start

  1. Calculate maintenance calories, then check them against your actual weight trend for two to three weeks.
  2. Set protein at 1.6 g per kg or more; fat around 25-35% of calories.
  3. Allow a weekly range rather than a single number: a weight band of about 1-2 kg is normal.
  4. Keep weighing yourself a few times a week so slow drift is caught early.

Common mistakes

Thinking maintenance means no structure

Without any tracking, weight usually drifts up slowly. A quick weekly check prevents this.

Expecting the scale to stay flat

Daily changes of 1-2 kg from water, salt and food volume are normal at maintenance.

Dropping protein

Protein needs do not disappear when you stop dieting; muscle maintenance still depends on it.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find my real maintenance?

Eat a consistent amount for two to three weeks. If your average weight does not change, that intake is your maintenance.

How long can I stay at maintenance?

Indefinitely. Many people spend most of the year at maintenance and only diet or bulk in short phases.

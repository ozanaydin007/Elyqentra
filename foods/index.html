How to use this table

Multiply each value by the weight you eat divided by 100. For example, 150 g of cooked chicken breast gives 165 × 1.5 ≈ 248 calories and 31 × 1.5 ≈ 46 g of protein. Weigh foods in the state listed: cooked rice and dry oats have very different values per 100 g.

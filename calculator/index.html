How it works

BMR is estimated with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, then multiplied by your activity level to get maintenance calories. Your goal and diet adjust calories and macros.

Calorie floors of 1,500 (men) and 1,200 (women) are applied. Very low intakes should only be followed under medical supervision.

What to do with your numbers

Treat the result as a starting point. Weigh yourself a few mornings a week, compare weekly averages, and adjust by 100-200 calories if the trend does not match your goal after two to three weeks.

Frequently asked questions

How is TDEE calculated?

BMR comes from the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, one of the most accurate formulas for most people. It is multiplied by an activity factor to estimate total daily energy expenditure.

How accurate is a calorie calculator?

Expect it to be within roughly 10% for most people. Your real-world weight trend is the final judge.

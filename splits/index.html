Workout splits

A workout split is how you divide your training across the week. The right one depends on how many days you can train, your experience and your goal. Each split below has complete workouts you can follow today or load straight into the tracker.

Push Pull Legs

Train pushing muscles, pulling muscles and legs on separate days, twice a week each.

6 days/weekMaximum muscle growth with high weekly volume

Upper / Lower

Alternate upper-body and lower-body sessions, four training days per week.

4 days/weekBalanced muscle and strength gains on four days

Full Body

Train the whole body in every session, three non-consecutive days per week.

3 days/weekBeginners, busy schedules and fat loss

Bro Split

One major muscle group per day: chest, back, shoulders, arms and legs.

5 days/weekBodybuilding-style volume, one muscle group per day

Chest/Biceps, Back/Triceps, Legs/Shoulders

Pair chest with biceps and back with triceps, then a legs and shoulders day, on a rolling four-day cycle.

4 days/weekArm-focused hypertrophy with antagonist pairing

Arnold Split

Chest and back together, shoulders and arms together, then legs, each twice a week.

6 days/weekHigh-volume mass building for experienced lifters

PHUL (Power Hypertrophy Upper Lower)

Two heavy power days and two higher-rep hypertrophy days on an upper/lower structure.

4 days/weekGetting stronger and bigger at the same time

5x5 Strength

Two alternating barbell workouts of five sets of five, three days a week.

3 days/weekRaw strength on the big barbell lifts

Hybrid Training

Strength training and running in one week: three lifting days, three conditioning days.

6 days/weekBeing strong and fit at the same time

HYROX Training

Running, station work and compromised running to prepare for a HYROX race.

5 days/weekPreparing for a HYROX fitness race

How to choose a split

If you can train three days a week, choose Full Body or 5x5 Strength. With four days, Upper / Lower or PHUL are hard to beat. With five or six days and good recovery, Push Pull Legs, the Bro Split or the Arnold Split give you the most volume. If you also run or race, pick Hybrid Training or HYROX Training.

The best split is the one you will actually follow for months. Consistency beats the perfect program.
